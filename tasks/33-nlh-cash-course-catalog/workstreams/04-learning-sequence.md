# 04 Learning Sequence

## Status

`done`

## Goal

Draft the recommended learning sequence for `NLH cash`.

## Scope

- beginner-to-advanced ordering;
- risk gates;
- review loops;
- practical session rhythm;
- later route presets.

## Requirements

- Start with bankroll/risk, rules, and math.
- Teach preflop before detailed postflop.
- Teach flop before turn/river complexity.
- Teach exploit and GTO after basic ranges and street logic.
- Introduce review routine early and reinforce it later.
- Do not imply advanced modules should be used in real play before risk basics.

## Suggested Macro Order

1. Вход и безопасность
2. Базовая математика
3. Preflop-ядро
4. Flop
5. Turn
6. River
7. 3-bet / 4-bet pots
8. Exploit
9. GTO/solver
10. Профессиональная рутина

## Done When

- Every course has an order hint.
- The sequence protects learners from jumping into advanced plays without foundations.
- The sequence can drive route presets later.
