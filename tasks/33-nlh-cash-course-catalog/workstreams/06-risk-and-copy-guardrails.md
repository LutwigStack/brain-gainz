# 06 Risk And Copy Guardrails

## Status

`done`

## Goal

Define safety and copy rules for the `NLH cash` campaign.

## Scope

- responsible copy;
- forbidden claims;
- risk notes;
- UI wording;
- progression guardrails.

## Requirements

- No guaranteed profit claims.
- No "easy money" framing.
- No real-money tracking in this epic.
- XP/mastery must mean learning progress, not financial result.
- Bankroll/risk and tilt controls should be visible early.
- Shot-taking and moving-down content must be framed conservatively.
- The campaign should stay suitable as educational software.

## Done When

- Agents have concrete copy rules before implementation.
- Browser QA can check for gambling-promo copy.
- Risk notes are part of course metadata.
