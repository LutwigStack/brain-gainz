# 05 Archive And System Content

## Status

`done`

The UI implementation demonstrates the archive and system-content behavior below.

## Goal

Make archive and system/developer content safe and secondary.

## Scope

- archive section or disclosure under personal campaigns
- archive copy explains that progress is kept
- restore action has clear label
- system/developer main campaign does not compete with learner-ready courses
- developer/system templates may be hidden behind author/dev details if appropriate

## Done When

- archive feels reversible, not destructive
- ordinary learner does not confuse system campaign with a course to start

## Post-UI Close Check

- Archive and restore affordances are secondary and clearly reversible.
- System/developer content does not compete with learner-ready courses.
